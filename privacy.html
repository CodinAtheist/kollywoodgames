1. Who we are

The Service is operated by Bilet Kollywood Games Private Limited, a company incorporated in India (CIN: U62011TZ2026PTC040493) with its registered office in Coimbatore 641050, Tamil Nadu, India.

2. Information we collect

We try to collect only what we need to run the game.

Account and sign-in information

When you sign in, we use Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method you choose ([Confirm sign-in methods, e.g. Google sign-in, phone number]), we receive a unique user ID and basic details such as your name, email address, phone number or profile picture.

Profile information

The display name and avatar you choose in the app, and any other profile details you add, such as your city or district if you choose to share it.

Gameplay information

Your answers, scores, points, rounds played, streaks, leaderboard positions and similar game activity.

Device and technical information

Device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash reports and diagnostic data, and identifiers such as app instance IDs and push notification tokens.

Advertising information

Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads. See third-party services below.

Location

[Confirm whether the app uses location. If it does:] With your permission, we may use your approximate location to show you local leaderboards. You can turn this off in your device settings at any time. We do not track your location in the background.

Information you send us

If you email us or contact support, we keep the messages and contact details you share so we can respond.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and save your progress across devices.
  • To run the game, including quizzes, scores and leaderboards.
  • To run weekly rewards for top players and to contact you if you win one.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.
  • To send you notifications about new questions, results and updates (you can turn these off in the app or in your device settings).
  • To keep the game fair and secure, including detecting cheating, abuse and fraud.
  • To understand how the game is used, fix bugs and improve the Service.
  • To respond to your messages and to meet our legal obligations.

5. Leaderboards and public information

Leaderboards are visible to other players. Your display name, avatar, score and rank may appear on them. If you share a city or district, that may appear on local leaderboards too. Please do not use your full real name or any sensitive information as your display name if you do not want it to be seen by others.

6. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the service providers listed above, to run the Service.
  • When you win a reward, with a partner that helps us deliver it, limited to what is needed to deliver it.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players, the public or us.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

7. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period after that where needed for legal, security or fraud prevention reasons.

You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by [using the delete account option in the app's settings, or] emailing kollywoodgameofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". We will confirm and complete the request within [30] days, except for information we must keep by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update your information.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, for example for notifications, location or personalised ads.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
