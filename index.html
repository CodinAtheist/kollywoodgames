Meet Enna Scene-uh

You see an illustrated scene from a famous Tamil movie. You get four options. Pick the right movie before the timer runs out.

Every scene is original artwork, generated using AI from scratch to represent our Tamil cinema movies. Some are easy to guess, while some will really test your Kollywood IQ. It is the quickest way to find out who is the real movie buff in your gang.

  • Illustrated movie scenes. Iconic moments, redrawn as original art.
  • Four options, one answer. Trust your gut and tap fast.
  • Quick rounds. Play a round in a couple of minutes, anywhere.

About us

The people behind the game

Kollywood Games is built by Bilet Kollywood Games Private Limited, a startup based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

We are a small team of Tamil cinema lovers and game makers. We grew up debating about cinema, humming songs and arguing over favourite scenes, and we are building the games we always wanted to play with our friends.
