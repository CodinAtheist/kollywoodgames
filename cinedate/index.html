Dating for movie lovers

Not another dating app

No endless swiping. No fake profiles. CineDate introduces you to people who love the same films, actors, directors and music as you, and then gets the two of you to an actual movie date.

Why we built it

Most dating apps show you a face and ask for a yes or no in two seconds. We think a better question is: would you two enjoy the same film on a Friday night? If you both rank the same directors, argue about the same actors and hum the same soundtracks, you already have something to talk about. CineDate starts there.

We are a small team in Coimbatore who love Tamil cinema. We built the app we wanted to use ourselves.

How it works

  1. Sign up with your email. You get a code by email, no password to remember. Your phone number is asked once, only to keep one account per person. It is never shown to anyone.
  2. Record a short selfie video. You say your name and a code on screen. Our team checks it before your profile can match. This is how we keep fake profiles out.
  3. Rank your cinema taste. Your top films, actors, directors, music and more. The more honest you are, the better your matches.
  4. Get suggestions with a match score. Each card tells you why: "You both rank Ilaiyaraaja #1", "3 favourite movies in common".
  5. One chat at a time. When a request is accepted, a chat opens. When you both feel ready, tap "Fix a Date", pick a day, and go watch a film together.

Hero, Heroine, Star

Inside CineDate, men are Heroes, women are Heroines and transpersons are Stars. It is a small thing, but it sets the tone: everyone here is the lead of their own story, and everyone deserves the same respect.

Before you tap download

  • This is a beta. Things may break, and we will fix them quickly. If something looks wrong, email us and tell us what happened.
  • Android only for now, version 7.0 or newer. The file is about 65 MB. Your phone may ask you to allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser; that is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  • You must be 18 or older. Sign-up asks for your date of birth and blocks younger users.
  • Verification takes a little time. A real person reviews your selfie video, usually within a day. Until then you can set up your profile and your cinema taste.
  • It is free. There are no paid features in this beta.

Your data, our promise

All your data is kept safe and encrypted on the server. Your phone number, your date of birth, your exact location and your verification video are never shown to other users. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Keep it decent

Remember to keep this app a decent place for all Heroes, Heroines and Stars. Be kind in chats, be honest in your profile, and meet in public places. Every post is reviewed before it goes live, and every profile and chat has Report and Block. Accounts that make others uncomfortable are removed.
