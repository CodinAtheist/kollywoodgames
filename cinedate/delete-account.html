CineDate

Delete your account

You can delete your CineDate account and all of its data from inside the app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open CineDate and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (phone number) and profile.
  • Profile photos, verification video and post photos.
  • Cinema taste rankings and catalog suggestions.
  • Match requests, passes, blocks, chats, messages and date plans.
  • Posts, likes, notification settings and device push tokens.

If you were in an active chat, it is ended and your match is told that the account was deleted. Reports you filed or that were filed about you may be kept in anonymised form for safety and legal reasons, as described in the Privacy Policy.

If you cannot open the app

Email support@kollywoodgames.com from any address with the subject "Delete my CineDate account" and the phone number you signed up with. We will confirm the request with a one-time code sent to that number before deleting, and reply when it is done.
