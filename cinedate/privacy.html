1. Who we are

CineDate is operated by Bilet Kollywood Games Private Limited ("we", "us"), CIN U62011TZ2026PTC040493, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. This policy covers the CineDate mobile app and the CineDate pages on kollywoodgames.com. The Kollywood Games website and games have their own Privacy Policy.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address — used to sign in with a one-time code and to identify your account.
  • Phone number — stored so that each person can hold only one account. It is never verified by SMS, never shown to other users and never used to contact you.
  • Profile details — name, date of birth, gender, who you want to meet, city, a short "about" text, occupation details, and up to six profile photos.
  • Verification video — a 5 to 15 second selfie video in which you say your name and a code shown on screen. Used only to confirm you are a real person.
  • Cinema taste — your ranked lists of films, actors, directors, music and other categories, and any catalog suggestions you submit.
  • Match preferences — preferred age range and maximum distance.
  • Content — match requests, chat messages, date proposals, posts and photos you publish, likes, reports and blocks.
  • Support messages — anything you send to our support address.

Information collected automatically

  • Location — if you allow location access, we convert your position to a city and store coordinates rounded to two decimal places (about one kilometre). You can instead choose a city from a list. Exact location is never stored or shown.
  • Device push token — so we can send notifications about requests, messages, dates and account updates. You control which kinds you receive in Settings.
  • Activity timestamps — such as when you were last active, to keep the feed relevant.
  • Technical logs — error and request logs kept by our hosting provider for security and debugging.

CineDate does not use advertising identifiers, advertising networks or third-party analytics trackers.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To verify that profiles belong to real adults (video review by our team).
  • To compute match suggestions from cinema taste, age range, distance and gender preference.
  • To run chats, date planning, posts and notifications.
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms of Use, prevent abuse and keep the community safe.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. What other users can see

Only users whose own profile has been verified can view other profiles. They can see your name, age (not date of birth), city, photos, about text, occupation summary, your top three ranked items per category, approved posts, and the match score between you.

Never shown to other users: your email address, your phone number, date of birth, exact location or coordinates, your verification video, your full ranked lists, and your preferences.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf under their own security commitments:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage and server functions that host CineDate.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — delivery of push notifications to your device.
  • Email delivery provider — delivery of the one-time sign-in code to your email address.

We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect the safety of users.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Profile photos, verification videos and post photos are stored in private storage and served through short-lived signed links. Database access rules are enforced on the server, so the app can only read what the signed-in user is allowed to see. Verification videos are accessible only to our moderation team.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete account (Settings → Delete account, or see this page) permanently deletes your login (email and phone number), profile, photos, verification video, rankings, requests, chats, messages, date plans, posts and device tokens. Any active chat is ended and your match is informed that the account was deleted.
  • Reports you filed about others, and reports filed about you, may be retained in anonymised form for safety and legal purposes.
  • Backups and server logs held by our hosting provider are cleared on their rolling schedule.

8. Your choices

  • Edit your photos, about text, city, occupation and preferences in the app at any time.
  • Turn each kind of notification on or off in Settings.
  • Block or report any user from their profile or chat.
  • Request a copy or correction of your data, or ask questions, at kollywoodgameofficial@gmail.com.

9. Age

CineDate is only for people aged 18 or older. Sign-up is blocked for younger dates of birth. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor we delete it.

10. Changes

We will post updates to this policy on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced inside the app.

11. Contact and grievance officer

For privacy questions, data requests or complaints under Indian law, write to our Grievance Officer at kollywoodgameofficial@gmail.com . We aim to acknowledge every request within 48 hours.
